## Michael

Write a 3-email founders welcome sequence for Byline (bylineseo.com) — an AI-powered SEO content platform. The sequence goes to people who just purchased during the founders sale (tiers: Starter $49/mo, Growth $99/mo, Multi-Brand $249/mo).

**Context about the product:**
- Byline generates SEO-optimized articles with a multi-pass research pipeline (GPT-4o for generation)
- After generation, an editorial agent (powered by Claude Sonnet) reviews the article and scores it across SEO, Readability, GEO, AEO
- Three agent modes: Review (editorial feedback), Assist (surgical rewrites on selected text), Auto (applies all fixes end-to-end)
- Users set up a brand profile first — voice, audience, topics, competitors — which personalizes every article
- Keyword research and discovery tools built in (DataForSEO powered)
- Articles can be managed as draft → brief_ready → complete → published

**Brand voice:** Direct, confident, editorial. Not hype-y. Byline is for content teams and solo SEOs who are serious about producing content that ranks. Copper/cream/ink visual palette. Think "the tool serious writers use."

**The sequence:**

**Email 1 — Welcome (send immediately on purchase)**
- Welcome them as a founding member
- What they unlocked (position it around the agent + content quality, not just "AI writer")
- One clear CTA: set up your brand profile (this is step 1, everything downstream depends on it)
- IMPORTANT: Include this note naturally — "As a founding member, your agent access is unlimited. We're waiving the standard session cap while we build with early users like you."

**Email 2 — Getting your first article (send Day 2)**
- Walk them through the workflow: brand profile → keyword research → brief → generate → agent review
- Tip: the agent scores every article — don't skip the Review step, it's where the value is
- One CTA: generate your first article

**Email 3 — Make it yours (send Day 5)**
- Focus on the brand profile and how to get more out of it (add competitors, tone examples, USPs)
- Mention the Assist mode for surgical rewrites
- Soft ask: reply to this email with what you're trying to rank for — position Michael as reachable
- One CTA: update your brand profile

**Format:** Write each email as a plain-text style email (minimal formatting, no heavy HTML). Subject line + preheader + body. Conversational but purposeful. From: Michael at Byline. Keep them short — founders are busy. No filler, no corporate speak.
