## Autoseo with AI — Competitive Summary

**What they sell:** Pure SaaS, not an agency. You give them your URL; they publish 1 AI-written SEO article per day (30/month, 1,500–2,500 words) + build 100 DA worth of backlinks — all on autopilot. Zero human involvement required from the customer. Plans run $49/mo (Starter), $99/mo (Pro), $199/mo (Scale), with a 30-day money-back guarantee on annual plans. There's also a WordPress plugin for auto-publishing directly to your site.

**Web traffic:** SimilarWeb timed out and no public traffic estimates surfaced. They claim 2,479+ active businesses and a "216% avg traffic increase." Take those self-reported numbers skeptically — they're marketing copy.

**Facebook Ads:** Their page ("Autoseo with AI") has 3,294 followers and 1,768 people currently talking about it — a high engagement ratio that confirms heavy, active paid promotion. The Facebook page ID (61582661703723) launched in 2024 based on the profile number, suggesting roughly 1–2 years of continuous ad spend. Could not pull a live ad count from the Ad Library without browser access, but the engagement volume signals 10+ active creatives running.

**Revenue signals:** Founded 2023 by Peter Zaborszky (London). Tracxn lists 1 employee — this is a solo-founder SaaS. 46 Trustpilot reviews at 4 stars. Tiny footprint: no Capterra/G2 presence for this specific product. Early-stage but actively scaling via paid social.

**Positioning vs. Byline:** AutoSEO owns the "totally hands-off, $49/mo, no skill required" lane. Their current hook is *"dominate ChatGPT, Perplexity AND Google"* — leaning hard into AI search anxiety. They're selling to SMB owners who want SEO results without thinking about it. Their weakness: it's a black box. Quality control, brand voice, and editorial oversight are nonexistent at this price point — which is exactly where Byline can differentiate on trust, control, and human judgment.
